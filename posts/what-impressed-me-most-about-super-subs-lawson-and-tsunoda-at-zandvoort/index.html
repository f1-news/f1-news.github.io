'What impressed me most about Lawson & Tsunoda in Zandvoort'

2026年8月31日 · 1 分钟 · 78 字

Last weekend’s Dutch Grand Prix had an air of finality to it, as the world of Formula 1 arrived knowing that, at least for the foreseeable future, Grand Prix cars will not return to the seaside circuit.

That said, triumphant returns were still the story of the weekend, in the shape of Liam Lawson and Yuki Tsunoda. Both drivers were being given an opportunity to return to their previous squads for one last - for now - hurrah.
